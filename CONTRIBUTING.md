# Contributing to OSS Policies

This repository centralizes policies for 3 Leaps and our supported OSS projects. We welcome suggestions to keep them fair, clear, and effective—see [README.md](README.md) for an overview.

## How to Contribute
- **Minor Suggestions**: Open an issue with details and rationale.
- **Major Changes**: Discuss in an issue first, then submit a pull request (PR) with your proposed updates.
- **Guidelines**: Ensure changes align with our [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [TRADEMARK-POLICY.md](TRADEMARK-POLICY.md), and overall repo purpose. All contributions are reviewed collaboratively.

For questions, contact legal@3leaps.net or open an issue.
